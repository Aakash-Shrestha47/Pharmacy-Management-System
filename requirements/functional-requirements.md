# Functional Requirements

| ID    | Category                 | Requirement                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| FR 1  | User Authentication      | The system shall allow authorized pharmacy staff to log in using individual usernames and passwords and shall provide access according to their assigned role.                  |
| FR 2  | Medicine Management      | The system shall allow authorized pharmacists to add, update, view, and remove medicine records, including medicine name, category, quantity, price, expiry date, and supplier. |
| FR 3  | Inventory Management     | The system shall allow authorized pharmacy staff to view current medicine quantities and inventory information.                                                                 |
| FR 4  | Low Stock Alert          | The system shall identify medicines below their minimum stock level and display a low-stock notification to authorized pharmacy staff.                                          |
| FR 5  | Medicine Search          | The system shall allow pharmacy staff to search for medicines by medicine name or category and display matching records.                                                        |
| FR 6  | Prescription Management  | The system shall allow authorized pharmacists to record, view, and update customer prescription information.                                                                    |
| FR 7  | Sales Processing         | The system shall allow authorized cashiers to record medicine sales, calculate the total amount, and update inventory quantities after a successful transaction.                |
| FR 8  | Bill Generation          | The system shall generate a bill containing the purchased medicines, quantities, prices, and total amount after a successful sale.                                              |
| FR 9  | Supplier Management      | The system shall allow authorized pharmacy managers to add, view, update, and remove supplier information.                                                                      |
| FR 10 | Reports                  | The system shall allow authorized pharmacy managers to view sales and inventory reports for monitoring pharmacy operations.                                                     |
| FR 11 | Staff Account Management | The system shall allow administrators to create, view, update, and deactivate staff accounts.                                                                                   |
| FR 12 | Role Management          | The system shall allow administrators to assign user roles and restrict access to functions based on the assigned role.                                                         |
